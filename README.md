# AI999X desktop

31 scientific models in one native app for Windows and Linux. Pick a capability, fill in the form (prefilled with a worked example) and press Compute: the answer with its units, a plain-language explanation and live figures. Everything runs on your computer; the app makes no network connection.

## Download

From the [releases](https://github.com/AI999X/ai999x-desktop/releases) or [www.ai999x.com/downloads/desktop/latest/](https://www.ai999x.com/downloads/desktop/latest/).

Windows: unzip and double-click `ai999x.exe` (no installer). Linux: extract and run `./ai999x`.
Check a download against `SHA256SUMS` (`sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS`).

Keyboard: Ctrl+K search, Enter compute. `ai999x --selftest` computes every worked example.
