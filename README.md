# AI999X desktop

39 scientific models in one native app for Windows and Linux. Each model also comes in a desktop app of its own (`ai999x-<model>-*`). Pick a capability, fill in the form (prefilled with a worked example) and press Compute: the answer with its units, a plain-language explanation and live figures. Everything runs on your computer; the app makes no network connection.
